Neuro Link — Privacy policy

Who provides this app

Neuro Link (com.discoveryscience.neurolink) is provided by Discovery Science, Inc. Contact montgomery@discovery-science.com for privacy questions. Effective date: October 5, 2026.

What Neuro Link does

Neuro Link connects to a Neurosity Crown EEG headset that you own, plays feedback tones when a selected brain state is reached, and can share that live state with other apps on your phone that you choose to install. It is a practice and self-study tool, not a medical device.

Neurosity account and EEG data

To use a Crown you sign in with your Neurosity account email and password. Sign-in, device selection and the EEG-derived streams (calm and focus scores, frequency band power and signal quality) are handled by Neurosity's official software development kit, which communicates with Neurosity's servers. That data is processed by Neurosity, Inc. under its own privacy policy and terms. Discovery Science does not operate a server for Neuro Link and does not receive your Neurosity credentials or EEG data. Your password is passed to the Neurosity SDK for sign-in and is not saved by Neuro Link; the SDK may keep a sign-in session in the app's private storage so you stay connected. Demo mode uses synthetic data and does not contact Neurosity.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your Neurosity email address, optional device ID, training settings and session recordings (CSV files with per-second scores, band values, events and markers) in app-private storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to Discovery Science.

Sharing with other apps

While Neuro Link is running it broadcasts live state (in-state flag, preset, calm, focus and relative band values) to apps on your phone that hold the Neuro Link receive-state permission, and accepts session markers from apps that hold the send-markers permission. Only install companion apps you trust; each app handles what it receives under its own policy.

Exports

When you choose Share on a saved session, Android lets you pick where the CSV file goes (for example email or a cloud drive). The destination you choose handles the file under its own policy.

Advertising, analytics and sale of data

Neuro Link contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. We do not sell or rent data.

Permissions

Internet (required for the Neurosity connection), foreground service and wake lock (so feedback continues while you use other apps), and notifications (the ongoing session notification). The app does not request location, microphone, camera, contacts or broad storage access.

Retention and deletion

Session files stay on your phone until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android automatic backup is disabled. Copies you shared elsewhere must be deleted at that destination. To delete your Neurosity account data, use Neurosity's own account tools or contact Neurosity.

Children

Neuro Link is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect children's data.

Policy changes

Any material change to how Neuro Link handles data will be reflected here with a new effective date. This policy covers Neuro Link only.
