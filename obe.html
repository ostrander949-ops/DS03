I · The experience

Leaving the body, as people describe it

An out-of-body experience is the vivid sense of perceiving the world from a location outside your own physical body. People who report one describe a recognisable run of events: a heaviness as the body goes still, a buzzing or rushing “vibration” spreading through it, sometimes a roar or a high ringing, then a sensation of rising, rolling or floating free, and a view of the room, or of their own body, from somewhere it cannot be seen from. The return is often abrupt, with a jolt and a rush of ordinary bodily feeling.

The experience is far from rare, and it has been mapped many times: by the numbered ladder of states a 1970s American sound laboratory made famous, by the hypnagogic literature on the edge of sleep, by the yordei merkavah who “descended to the Chariot” with head between the knees, by the vǫlva on her high seat while the varðlokkur were sung. The research behind OBE Induction is on its own page.

OBE Induction follows the ritual logic those traditions share. It does not play you a recording and hope. It prepares you, orders the stages, holds you at the edge, and brings you back.

III · The core protocol

The Threshold progression

The central protocol climbs the old numbered ladder that every serious student of the out-of-body literature will recognise, the rungs once counted ten, twelve and fifteen. At Threshold I the body goes heavy and quiet while awareness stays clear. At Threshold II awareness widens beyond the boundary of the skin until the room itself is part of what you are calmly observing. At Threshold III you stop measuring time and rest in still, continuous awareness, the stage the guidance describes as “stop seeking an event.” Then a full sensory Return.

The beat drops as you go deeper, from the alpha border into the heart of theta, then climbs back for the return. The carrier tone moves with it, so each state has its own unmistakable sonic signature that you learn to recognise and, with practice, re-enter.

V · The ceremonial hours

The angel of this hour, under your own sky

The Ars Paulina, third book of the Lemegeton, gives a ruling angel to each of the twenty-four hours. Those hours are not clock hours. They are unequal “temporal” hours: daylight divided into twelve, night divided into twelve, so they stretch and shrink with the seasons and with where you stand.

OBE Induction computes them properly. With your permission it reads your location, calculates the true local sunrise and sunset, finds the current temporal hour and its planet in the traditional sequence, and names the angel of that hour from the Sloane manuscript table. A seal diagram turns at the centre of the screen. In the Celestial Alignment stage the compass guides you to face the azimuth of the ruling planet, even when it is below the horizon, with eyes lowered if it is the Sun.

The ceremony then runs in seven stages: purification, celestial alignment, planetary attunement, invocation, quiet audience, thanksgiving and license to depart, and grounding. The sound field darkens and quiets as you approach the audience, then opens again for the return. The voice warns you when the hour is about to change.

Ritual: the EEG edition

OBE Induction has an EEG counterpart, Ritual: the same twelve protocols, voice and landscapes, rebuilt around a live brain signal. It connects to a Neurosity Crown and streams relative band power, delta, theta, alpha, beta and gamma, through the whole session. Everything you mark is stamped with the state of your brain at that instant.

  • Live EEG sonification. The atmosphere responds to your relative band power. Whichever rhythm dominates, delta, theta, alpha, beta or gamma, shapes the sound field, so with eyes closed you hear the moment theta takes over.
  • Gamma events, defined strictly. Gamma is smoothed and compared with a rolling baseline built from your own signal, ready after thirty seconds and spanning up to two minutes. An event is logged only when it rises 1.5, 2 or 2.5 standard deviations above that baseline and holds for at least three-quarters of a second, with an eight-second gap before the next. Each one sounds a gamma harmonic in the field, and can be switched off.
  • Markers tied to the signal. Every OUT, IN, LIGHT or PEAK carries the five band powers and the gamma score from the same moment, and the time to the nearest gamma event before and after.
  • Ten seconds either side. For every marker the report compares each band in the ten seconds before with the ten seconds after.
  • Out and back, measured. Each OUT is paired with the next IN to give the duration of the excursion and the gamma level before, during and after it.
  • Honest about artifacts. Speech and muscle tension contaminate gamma. The report flags every event and marker that falls near a spoken word.

What a session looks like, phenomenologically

A session, read

Threshold progression, 30 minutes, lying down, eyes closed. An illustrative composite drawn from the kinds of sessions we log in our own practice; times and values are representative, not a transcript.

  1. 03:40Threshold I. Alpha falls as the eyes settle; theta begins to climb. Body reported as heavy and distant.

  4. 12:30Threshold II. The room “opens.” Awareness described as extending to the corners of the ceiling.
  5. 14:06OUT. A rolling sensation to the left; the bed seen from about head height beside it.
