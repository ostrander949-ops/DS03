Contact I

The Angels

Intelligences addressed by name, office and hour

Every angelic rite in the record rests on three facts: the angel has a name, a seal, and a time when it governs. The operator never calls an angel in general. He calls one, at the hour it rules, by the name the text gives, with its seal before him. These recreations rebuild that address exactly and calculate the hour for your coordinates, because in the sources the hour is the door. The state asked of the operator is long, devotional attention: prayer repeated until the mind is quiet, then the call.

Angelarium

ContactThe angel governing the present hour

The Heptameron; the Ars Paulina

What the app does

Identifies the ruling angel of the current planetary hour from your local sunrise and sunset, shows its name, office and seal, and leads the address in the order the Heptameron gives: preparation, invocation, request and dismissal. A forecast lists every coming hour each angel governs, so an operation can be planned days ahead.

Why it works

In the Heptameron each day and hour belongs to a planet, and that planet’s angels serve only within its window. Calling an angel outside its hour was considered pointless. The arithmetic that once took an operator an evening by candlelight now runs as a living clock, so the address can be made the moment the door opens.

What the app does

Presents the forty-eight Calls in the angelic language as Dee recorded them, with pronunciation and English sense, and leads their recitation in order. Each Call opens a Watchtower or one of the Aethyrs, and the app keeps track of where the practitioner stands in the sequence.

Why it works

Dee believed these words were dictated by angels through a scrying stone, and that they were the language Adam spoke in Eden. The Calls are dense, rhythmic and nearly impossible to follow with the reasoning mind. That is part of how they work: the recitation fills the speaking mind while attention sinks beneath it.

Contact II

The Throne and the Name

Ascent through guarded heavens to the presence of God

The Jewish mystical record describes contact as an ascent. The practitioner rises through seven palaces, or along the paths of the Tree, or toward the chariot Ezekiel saw, meeting guardians who demand a seal and a name at every stage. The Hekhalot texts are specific about the body: head lowered between the knees, hymns repeated until voice and breath become one rhythm. Read today, these are unmistakably instructions for leaving ordinary waking consciousness while staying fully awake.

Hekhalot: The Seven Palaces

ContactThe gatekeepers of the seven palaces, and the Throne

Hekhalot Rabbati; Hekhalot Zutarti; Maʿaseh Merkavah; 3 Enoch

What the app does

Guides the ascent palace by palace. At each gate the app presents the guardian, the seal to be shown and the name to be spoken, then carries the practitioner forward on the hymn. It ends before the Throne, followed by a deliberate, grounded return.

Why it works

The “descenders to the chariot” treated the ascent as exact and dangerous. A wrong name at a gate turned the traveller back. That exactness is what the recreation preserves. The long, repetitive hymns of the Hekhalot, sung in fixed cadence, are among the clearest cases in the whole record of language used as entrainment.

Contact III

The Gods

Daily service at the shrine, by epithet and offering

For three thousand years the temples of Sumer, Egypt and Greece performed contact as daily service. The god was woken, washed, clothed, fed and addressed in fixed words, at fixed hours, by people who held that the words themselves made the presence real. These recreations keep the epithets, the order of offering and the formulas, and set them inside the sound world the temples used: drum, sistrum, drone and voice.

Contact IV

Spirits and Jinn

The circle, the seal, and license to depart

The Solomonic tradition treats contact as a working relationship conducted under strict protection. The operator stands inside a drawn circle, the spirit is called by its seal into a triangle outside it, given its charge, and released by license to depart. The Arabic sources that reached Europe in translation add the planetary hour and the hexagram seal of Solomon. These rites ask for the most controlled state of all: deeply absorbed, and fully in command.

Contact V

Christ

Presence kept in the heart and through the hours

The Christian contemplatives sought contact as presence rather than apparition. The Desert Fathers joined the name of Jesus to the breath until the prayer said itself. The Benedictines broke the day into hours so that attention returned to God again and again. Both are training methods practised for a lifetime, and both aim at a settled awareness the hesychasts compared to still water in the heart.

The state

Theta dominance, gamma bursts.

Theta dominance is a stretch of time in which the slow theta rhythm, four to eight cycles a second, outweighs the faster rhythms of ordinary waking. It is the brain of the drum journey and the edge of sleep, with the practitioner still awake inside it. Every app in the collection is tuned to bring you there.

Gamma bursts are brief flashes of very fast, widely synchronised activity. Gamma is thought to help the brain bind separate perceptions into one experience, and a few studies hint that strong, brain-wide gamma may go with moments of heightened awareness and felt connection:

  • Lutz et al., PNAS, 2004Long-term Tibetan Buddhist practitioners produced unusually high-amplitude gamma synchrony while meditating on unconditional compassion.
  • Braboszcz et al., PLoS One, 2017Experienced meditators from three different traditions showed higher gamma amplitude than non-meditators.
  • Xu et al., PNAS, 2023In the brains of dying patients, researchers recorded surges of gamma activity and coupling in regions tied to conscious awareness, and suggested they may reflect a last burst of inner experience.

None of this proves contact. But it is a striking echo of what the rites describe at their summit: a mind let down almost completely and then, in a flash, brilliantly connected.

Ways in

Many doors, one threshold.

The drum, the name, the breath and the hour turn up from Sumer to the Desert Fathers to the modern sound laboratory. Each app combines the ones its sources used, and every one is carried by binaural sound. Each app above is marked with the ways in it uses.

Binaural entrainment

Δ 4–8 Hz · headphones

Two tones, one in each ear, a few hertz apart. The brain hears a third beat at the difference: 200 Hz in one ear and 206 Hz in the other give a 6 Hz theta pulse. It is a gentle push toward a rhythm, used under every rite here.

The drum

4–7 beats / s

A monotonous pulse at four to seven beats a second, the oldest entrainment instrument on earth. Ethnographers found it at the centre of trance rites on several continents.

Chant and drone

sustained pitch

Sustained vowels on one breath, single-pitch drones, and words the reasoning mind cannot follow: the seven vowels of the Greek Papyri, the Enochian Calls, Sumerian incantation.

Paced breath

≈ 6 breaths / min

Prayer joined to a slow breath until the two become one rhythm. The hesychast Jesus Prayer and the Sufi dhikr both settle near six breaths a minute.

The Name, repeated

litany · decree

The litany: a divine name or hymn repeated until it runs on its own. Repetition occupies the speaking mind completely while attention drops beneath it.

The seal and the gaze

single point

Fixed attention on a single drawn figure. Every grimoire puts a seal in front of the operator, and every recreation redraws it from the manuscript.

The hour

calculated to your sky

The rite opens at its proper moment: planetary hours from your local sunrise and sunset, the Moon, the night watches. In the sources, the hour is the door.

Ascent

staged visualisation

A fixed sequence of visualised stations: seven palaces, ten sefirot, the archons’ spheres, the wheels of the chariot. Each station gathers attention closer to one point.

Incubation

hypnagogic edge

Contact at sleep onset. The petitioner lies down expecting a visit and crosses into hypnagogia on purpose, the most theta-rich passage of an ordinary night.

Withdrawal

objectless

No name, no image, no goal. Neti neti, the cloud of forgetting, the gateless gate: every object is set aside until only awareness remains.
