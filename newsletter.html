The Angels of the Week · November 22–28, 2026

Seven angels rule the seven days in the old books, and twenty-four more keep the hours. Here is who is keeping this week, what the books say each can do, and when their hours are strongest. For the exact hours where you live, open the live clock: /angel-of-the-hour.html

The aligned hours. On every day, four hours belong to the same planet as the day itself: the first and eighth hours after sunrise and the third and tenth after sunset. Tradition counts them as the strongest hours for the day’s work. In the Ars Paulina they are always kept by the same four angels: Samael, Osmadiel, Serquanich and Iassuarim.

The Moon this week

  • Full moon: Tuesday November 24, 14:54 UTC

Card of the week: The World

From the Angelic Tarot, the card The World is given to Cassiel, angel of Saturn. Upright it speaks of completion, wholeness, fulfilment; reversed, of loose ends, delay in completion. Keep it in mind as a thread through the week.

Sunday November 22 · Michael, angel of the Sun

Prosperity · Favour · Honour · Health

Wealth and good fortune, the goodwill of others, the end of quarrels, honour and advancement, and health. The Heptameron says the spirits of Sunday have power “to procure Gold, Gemmes, Carbuncles, Riches; to cause one to obtain favour and benevolence; to dissolve the enmities of men; to raise men to honors; to carry or take away infirmities”.

An hour angel to meet: Osmadiel, who keeps the eighth hour of the day (the hour called Jafanym). Today he serves the Sun, so his hour carries the work of the Sun: prosperity · favour · honour · health. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-osmadiel

Reflection: Thank someone whose goodwill has helped you, out loud or in writing.

Monday November 23 · Gabriel, angel of the Moon

Journeys · Secrets revealed · Silver · Thought and memory

Safe and swift journeys, things carried where they need to go, secrets brought to light, and a clear, remembering mind. The Heptameron says the spirits of Monday have power “to give silver; to convey things from place to place; to make horses swift, and to disclose the secrets of persons both present and future”.

An hour angel to meet: Sabrathan, who keeps the first hour of the night (the hour called Omalharien). Today he serves Venus, so his hour carries the work of Venus: love · marriage · reconciliation · pleasure. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-sabrathan

Reflection: If you are travelling, ask for a safe road; if not, walk somewhere you have not walked before.

Tuesday November 24 · Samael, angel of Mars

Strength · Victory in conflict · Health given or taken

Force and victory in conflict, armed help, and power over illness. In the old books Mars is the planet of war, and its hours were the most feared. The Heptameron says the spirits of Tuesday have power “to cause wars, mortality, death and combustions; and to give two thousand Souldiers at a time; to bring death, infirmities or health”.

An hour angel to meet: Zaazenach, who keeps the sixth hour of the night (the hour called Thaazaron). Today he serves Mercury, so his hour carries the work of Mercury: knowledge · prophecy · justice · healing. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-zaazenach

Reflection: Move your body until you feel your strength, then ask for courage used well.

Wednesday November 25 · Raphael, angel of Mercury

Knowledge · Prophecy · Justice · Healing

Learning and lost knowledge recovered, foreknowledge, favour with judges, healing, the poor raised up, and locked things opened. The Heptameron says the spirits of Wednesday have power “to give all Metals; to reveal all earthly things past, present and to come; to pacifie judges, to give victories in war, to re-edifie, and teach experiments and all decayed Sciences, and to change bodies mixt of Elements conditionally out of one into another: to give infirmities or health; to raise the poor, and cast down the high ones; to binde or lose Spirits; to open locks or bolts”.

An hour angel to meet: Dardariel, who keeps the eleventh hour of the night (the hour called Aalacho). Today he serves the Moon, so his hour carries the work of the Moon: journeys · secrets revealed · silver · thought and memory. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-dardariel

Reflection: Write the letter or message you have been putting off.

Thursday November 26 · Sachiel, angel of Jupiter

Joy · Love · Peace · Healing

Joy and good cheer, love, peace in place of strife, enemies calmed, healing, and losses averted. The Heptameron says the spirits of Thursday have power “to procure the love of woman; to cause men to be merry and joyful; to pacifie strife and contentions; to appease enemies; to heal the diseased, and to disease the whole; and procureth losses, or taketh them away”.

An hour angel to meet: Vathmiel, who keeps the fourth hour of the day (the hour called Elechym). Today he serves Venus, so his hour carries the work of Venus: love · marriage · reconciliation · pleasure. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-vathmiel

Reflection: Make peace with one person, even if only in your own heart.

Friday November 27 · Anael, angel of Venus

Love · Marriage · Reconciliation · Pleasure

Love and desire, marriage, enemies reconciled, pleasure and laughter, and health. The Heptameron says the spirits of Friday have power “to give silver: to excite men, and incline them to luxury; to reconcile enemies through luxury; and to make marriages; to allure men to love women; to cause, or take away infirmities; and to do all things which have motion”.

An hour angel to meet: Quabriel, who keeps the ninth hour of the day (the hour called Karron). Today he serves Mercury, so his hour carries the work of Mercury: knowledge · prophecy · justice · healing. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-quabriel

Reflection: Make one thing more beautiful: a table, a corner of a room, a page.

Saturday November 28 · Cassiel, angel of Saturn

Endings · Binding · Severity

In the old books Saturn’s work is the hardest: discord, grief, cold and endings. Later astrology softened him into the planet of time, patience, discipline and limits. The Heptameron says the spirits of Saturday have power “to sow discordes, hatred, evil thoughts and cogitations; to give lead freely, to slay and kill every one, and to lame or maim every member”.

An hour angel to meet: Tartys, who keeps the second hour of the night (the hour called Panezur). Today he serves the Moon, so his hour carries the work of the Moon: journeys · secrets revealed · silver · thought and memory. /angel-of-the-hour.html#paulina-tartys

Reflection: Sit for ten minutes in silence and let what needs to end, end.
