The Library

Ashurbanipal gathered the rites of Mesopotamia onto clay at Nineveh. The scribes of Uruk kept the star lists and incantations. Alexandria tried to hold everything. This library keeps the same material in a new medium: each rite rebuilt as a working instrument, shelved by the tradition that wrote it down.

Every work here is a complete Android app with no ads, accounts or trackers. Each card shows whether the app is on Google Play, in review or still in development. The original rites also come in a Live Neurofeedback EEG edition that listens to your brain as you practise.
