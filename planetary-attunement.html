Planetary Attunement

The old planetary hours, calculated for where you are.

Planetary timing and guided contemplation with historical context. Enter your coordinates and the app divides your day and night into twelve unequal hours each, from your local sunrise and sunset, and pairs each planet with a short modern reflection.

Inside the app

What you can do

I

Calculated planetary hours

From manually entered coordinates and time zone, the app calculates sunrise and sunset and shows the current hour, the day’s ruling planet, when the hour ends and which comes next, with the next twelve hours listed.

II

Seven practices

Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn each have a short modern contemplation, timed in stages from 1 to 60 minutes with original ambient sound.

III

Optional reminders

Choose planets and receive an approximate reminder ten minutes before their hour. Android may delay reminders to save battery.

IV

A private journal

Sessions are recorded on your device, with editable reflections and JSON export. Coordinates never leave the phone.

History & boundaries

What comes from the record, and what is new

EVIDENCEFrom the historical record

  • Theurgy, in late antiquity, meant ritual work aimed at a relationship with the divine. Iamblichus defended it; modern research studies his arguments.
  • Renaissance writers such as Agrippa (Book I, chapters 23–29) assigned metals, colours and qualities to the planets. These correspondences are a later Western tradition, separate from late-antique theurgy.

MODERNMade for this app

  • The seven reflective sequences are modern. No universal ancient colour system or ritual is asserted.
  • The app makes no claim that planets influence you. Where there is no full sunrise–sunset cycle, such as polar day or night, it says the hours are undefined.

Sources named in the app: Agrippa, Three Books of Occult Philosophy I.23–29 · University of Liverpool research on Iamblichean theurgy

Planetary Attunement is a contemporary contemplative experience for study and reflection. It does not promise contact, prediction, initiation, healing or any other outcome, and it is not medical or psychological advice.
