Privacy

This website

discovery-science.com is a static website with no accounts, forms or advertising, and it sets no cookies. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits: it records which pages are viewed, the referring site, and the country, browser, operating system and device type, without cookies and without identifying individual visitors. Our host, Vercel, may also keep standard server logs such as IP address and request time for security and reliability. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and app screenshots and icons load from Google Play’s image servers.

Email

If you write to us, for example to order a Live Neurofeedback EEG edition, we use your address and message only to reply and to handle what you asked for. We do not sell or share it.

Our apps

Each Discovery Science app has its own privacy policy, linked from its Google Play listing. Our apps work without accounts, contain no ads or trackers, and keep journals and records on your device.

Live Neurofeedback EEG editions

EEG editions process signals from your headset on your own device to drive the experience. Session recordings stay on your device unless you choose to export them. Each EEG edition ships with its own privacy policy describing exactly how that data is handled.
