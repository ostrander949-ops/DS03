Apps for the rites of contact

Ancient rites of contact, rebuilt as apps.

For five thousand years, people kept exact procedures for reaching angels, gods, jinn and the dead: the hour, the names, the seals, the words and the sound. Each app reconstructs one of those rites from its primary sources, the cuneiform tablets, the Greek magical papyri, the Hekhalot manuscripts and the Solomonic grimoires, read with modern AI beside the scholarly translations, and keeps every step in the order the texts give it.

What you hold is the exact ritual, reconstructed with historical accuracy, as it was intended to summon presences: timed to your own sky, guided step by step, and carried on binaural sound tuned to the state each rite was built to produce. That state is theta dominance, measurable on EEG and trainable with practice, the threshold where every one of these traditions placed contact with the other side.

How every app works

The rite, the hour, the state.

The old rites were exact procedures, and they all asked for the same thing from the person performing them: a mind quieted to the edge of sleep and held there, awake. Each app is built to take you to that state the way its tradition did.

Rebuilt from the source

The names, seals, words and order are taken from the text that preserves the rite, then guided step by step so you can perform it as written.

Set to your sky

Planetary days and hours, the Moon and the night watches, calculated from sunrise and sunset where you are, so every rite opens at its proper moment.

Carried on binaural sound

Layered binaural tones, drum, drone and voice, tuned toward theta, stand in for the temple and the chant. Put on headphones and the rite carries you.
