Discovery Science · OBE Induction

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how Discovery Science, Inc. handles information in OBE Induction (the “App”), package com.discoveryscience.ritual. The App offers guided audio meditation and contemplative practices, optional location-based timing, voice markers, and a local session journal.

Information processed by the App

Location. If you choose a location-based feature, Android may provide the App with your precise or approximate location. The App uses it on your device to calculate local sunrise and sunset, planetary-hour timing, and related practice context. Location-derived details, including coordinates, may be saved with a session in your local practice history.

Microphone and voice markers. Voice markers are optional. If you enable them, the App uses the speech-recognition service available on your phone to recognize selected English practice markers. The App records recognized marker words and their timing in session notes; it does not intentionally record or save raw microphone audio. Speech-service processing is subject to the service provider’s own settings and privacy terms. Touch markers can be used without microphone access.

Practice history and journal. Session details, program selections, elapsed time, optional location-derived context, touch or voice markers, accessibility and audio preferences, and journal reflections are stored locally on your device. The App does not provide an account or cloud-sync feature and does not send this practice history or journal content to Discovery Science.

Sharing, analytics, and advertising

Discovery Science does not sell your personal information. The current App build does not include advertising or an analytics feature. The App requests network access for its Android web-app environment and may open external source links. When you visit an external website or use a device-provided speech-recognition service, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy. The App does not send session history, journal entries, or location coordinates to Discovery Science servers.

Retention and deletion

Practice history, settings, markers, and journal entries remain in local app storage on your device until you delete them. You can remove App data using Android Settings or uninstall the App. No account is created, so there is no server-side account or profile to delete. A speech-recognition provider may retain or process information under its separate terms and settings.

Security

Local information is protected by the security controls available on your Android device and may be accessible to anyone with access to the unlocked device or its app data. Use the device’s screen lock and keep Android up to date. Avoid entering sensitive personal, medical, or confidential information in journal entries.

Children

OBE Induction is not designed for children under 13, and Discovery Science does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

Changes to this policy

If the App’s information practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised. The current version is available at this URL.
