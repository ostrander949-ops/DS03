Discovery Science · APKALLU

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Discovery Science handles information in the APKALLU Android application (“the App”). The App provides educational and research material about Apkallu traditions, ancient Mesopotamian ritual texts, and related scholarship.

Information the App collects

The current APKALLU release does not request access to personal information or device features, does not require an account, and does not include advertising or analytics functionality. The App does not collect or transmit personal data to Discovery Science. Its reference material and media are included with the App and are used on your device.

Information stored on your device

Android and the App may store basic technical preferences or app state locally so the App can function. This information is not transmitted to Discovery Science. You can remove locally stored App data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Links to other websites

The App may provide links to external sources for research and reference. If you open a third-party website, that site’s operator may collect information under its own privacy policy. Discovery Science does not control those websites or their data practices.

Children’s privacy

The App is an educational reference app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, Discovery Science does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the App.

Security and changes

Because the App does not transmit personal data to Discovery Science, we do not maintain a server-side profile of App users. If the App’s features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant release. The latest version will be available at this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the App, contact hello@discovery-science.com.
