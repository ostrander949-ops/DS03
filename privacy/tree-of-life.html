Discovery Science · Privacy policy

Tree of Life: Kabbalah

Android app com.discoveryscience.treeoflife · Last updated October 3, 2026

Summary

Tree of Life: Kabbalah does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

If you use the journal, save favourites, or complete a session, the app stores that information only in its private storage on your device: journal text, favourites, session times, stages, markers, and any notes or ratings you choose to add, together with your app settings. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

Export and deletion

In Settings you can export your data to a file you choose, and you can delete all of it at once. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup, so your journal is not copied to any server by the app.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It does not use the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions. Sound is generated on the device.

EEG

The app's session format is designed so that a future edition could record EEG readings alongside a session. This version does not connect to any EEG device and records no physiological data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Links

The About Discovery Science screen can open other Discovery Science apps in Google Play. Google Play has its own privacy policy.
