Chart of the Hour — Privacy policy

Package: com.discoveryscience.chartofthehour

App: Chart of the Hour

Developer: Discovery Science

Contact: Montgomery@discovery-science.com

Effective date: 2026-10-03

Chart of the Hour collects nothing. There is no account, no advertisements, and no analytics. The app does not use the network and does not use GPS.

Birth date, birth time, and the city you pick are stored only in local preferences on the device. They are not sent to Discovery Science or to anyone else. The city list, including latitude, longitude, and time zone, is built into the app. Choosing a city does not look up your location.

Uninstalling the app removes that local birth data.
