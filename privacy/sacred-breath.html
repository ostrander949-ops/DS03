Sacred Breath — Privacy policy

Sacred Breath (com.discoveryscience.sacredbreath) is provided by Discovery Science, Inc. Effective October 4, 2026. Contact montgomery@discovery-science.com for privacy questions.

Information stored on your device

Session history, reflections, settings and an interrupted-session checkpoint are stored in app-private Android storage. No account, ads, analytics, cloud database, microphone recording, or developer server is used. No personal information is sold or automatically transmitted to us. The app has no INTERNET permission.

Exports

Exporting CSV uses the Android document picker only after you choose Export. Your selected destination may be a cloud provider governed by its own policy. Exported files include your history and reflections. We cannot delete separately exported copies.

Retention and deletion

Delete individual entries in History; clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove local app data. Android cloud backup and device transfer of app data are disabled. Storage is protected by Android's sandbox and device security; the app does not provide its own encrypted vault.

Permissions and third parties

No dangerous permissions are requested. Android system services provide the document picker, optional view haptics and optional locally generated sound cues. No Google analytics or advertising SDK is included. Google Play may separately process purchase/download information under its policies.

Children and support email

The app is intended for adults. It has no child-directed accounts or server collection. If you email support, we receive the address and message you choose to send; these are used to respond and may be deleted on request subject to applicable obligations.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected in this policy.
