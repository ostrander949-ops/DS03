Hour of the Rite — Privacy policy

Package: com.discoveryscience.houroftherite

Developer: Discovery Science
Contact: Montgomery@discovery-science.com
Effective date: 2026-10-03

Hour of the Rite is an offline Android app from Discovery Science. This policy describes that app only.

The app collects nothing. It does not ask you to create an account. It does not show ads. It does not use analytics. It does not use the network. It does not request location permission and it does not read GPS.

You can choose a city from a list stored in the app. That choice is saved in local preferences on your device and stays on the device. Discovery Science does not receive it.

The app shares nothing and sells nothing. There is no server, no account data, and no advertising or analytics identifier.

If you contact Discovery Science by email, that message is handled as ordinary email and is not collected by the app itself.

Questions about this policy: Montgomery@discovery-science.com.
