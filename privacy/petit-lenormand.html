Petit Lenormand — Privacy policy

Package: com.discoveryscience.petitlenormand

Developer: Discovery Science
Contact: Montgomery@discovery-science.com
Effective date: 2026-10-03

Petit Lenormand is an offline Android app from Discovery Science. This policy describes that app only.

The app is offline. It collects nothing. It does not ask you to create an account. It does not show ads. It does not use analytics. It does not use the network.

A draw you save, including an optional question, the time, the card numbers, and the places, stays on the device. Discovery Science does not receive it. Uninstalling the app removes that local data.

The app shares nothing and sells nothing. There is no server and no advertising or analytics identifier.

If you contact Discovery Science by email, that message is handled as ordinary email and is not collected by the app itself.

Questions about this policy: Montgomery@discovery-science.com.
