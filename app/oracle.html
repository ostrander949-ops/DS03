ORACLE

A divination app that reads the present moment through Tarot, the I Ching and the Elder Futhark, joined to the planetary hour.

What the app does

Reads the present moment through seventy-eight Tarot cards, sixty-four hexagrams and twenty-four runes, joined to the planetary hour and its angel, with a journal that keeps the record.

Ways in: The hour, The seal and the gaze. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

Divination is the oldest way of asking a question of something beyond yourself and waiting for the answer. Every system here was built for that exchange.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
Tarot; the I Ching; the Elder Futhark; planetary and angelic correspondences. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
