What the app does

Finds the planet assigned to an intention and locates it in the real sky by compass. Shows the planetary day and hour, the Mesopotamian and Renaissance invocations, and live readings of the geomagnetic field.

Ways in: The hour, The seal and the gaze. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The Picatrix is clear that a planet is addressed where it stands, facing it. The phone becomes the instrument the astrologer-magician needed: a clock of hours and a pointer to the sky.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Picatrix; Babylonian astral prayer. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
