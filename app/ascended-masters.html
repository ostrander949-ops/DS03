What the app does

Addresses the masters and the rays of the theosophical tradition through invocation, decree and visualisation.

Ways in: The Name, repeated, Chant and drone, Ascent. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The decree, a short affirmation spoken aloud and repeated many times, is the modern descendant of the litany. Its repetition does to the mind what the Hekhalot hymns did.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
Theosophical and later teachings. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
