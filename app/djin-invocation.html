Djin Invocation

An Android app that recreates the Arabic rite of calling the jinn under Solomon’s seal, at the hour of its planet.

What the app does

Sets the circle, the hexagram seal and the planetary hour, then leads the call, the witnessing and the release in the order of the Arabic manuals.

Ways in: The seal and the gaze, The hour, The Name, repeated, Binaural entrainment. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

In Islamic cosmology the jinn are a real creation, made of smokeless fire and living alongside humans. The magical manuals treated contact with them as possible, risky and governable by Solomon’s seal. The recreation keeps the protective structure whole, because in the sources the structure is the rite.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
Ghāyat al-Ḥakīm (the Picatrix); the Testament of Solomon. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
