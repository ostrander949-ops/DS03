What the app does

Leads the practitioner through Action, Formation, Creation and Emanation, the four worlds of Lurianic Kabbalah, with the name and inner posture proper to each.

Ways in: Ascent, The Name, repeated, Withdrawal. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

Here contact is a change of depth. The same prayer said in the world of Action and in the world of Emanation is understood to reach a different level of reality. The recreation makes that inward descent explicit, world by world.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
Lurianic Kabbalah. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
