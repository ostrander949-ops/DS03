The Witness

What the app does

The Advaita Vedanta practice in three movements. Neti neti, not this, not this: every sensation, thought and state is noticed and set aside. Self-enquiry: attention turns back to ask who is aware. Then rest as the witness, with no object at all.

Ways in: Withdrawal, Paced breath. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

Advaita teaches that the self, ātman, and the absolute, brahman, are one. Nothing needs to be reached, because nothing was ever separate. The Upanishads name four states, waking, dreaming, deep sleep and turīya, the fourth, which is present through the other three. No tradition comes closer to describing from the inside the state these instruments read from the outside.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Upanishads; Śaṅkara; Ramana Maharshi’s self-enquiry. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
