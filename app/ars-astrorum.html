Ars Astrorum

A planetary hours calculator that divides your local day and night into the unequal hours of the Chaldean order.

ContactThe rulers of the hours

The Chaldean order of the planets

Find it on Google PlayAndroid · binaural edition · EEG add-on coming

What the app does

Calculates the unequal planetary hours from your local sunrise and sunset and shows which planet rules each one.

Ways in: The hour. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The Chaldean order, Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, gave the week its days and every magical operation its timing. It is the foundation the rest of the timing rests on.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Chaldean order of the planets. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
