Angelarium

An Android app that tells you which angel governs the present planetary hour, with its name, seal and the Heptameron’s order of address.

The Heptameron; the Ars Paulina

What the app does

Identifies the ruling angel of the current planetary hour from your local sunrise and sunset, shows its name, office and seal, and leads the address in the order the Heptameron gives: preparation, invocation, request and dismissal. A forecast lists every coming hour each angel governs, so an operation can be planned days ahead.

Ways in: The hour, The seal and the gaze, The Name, repeated, Binaural entrainment. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

In the Heptameron each day and hour belongs to a planet, and that planet’s angels serve only within its window. Calling an angel outside its hour was considered pointless. The arithmetic that once took an operator an evening by candlelight now runs as a living clock, so the address can be made the moment the door opens.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Heptameron; the Ars Paulina. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
