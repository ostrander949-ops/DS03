Solomonic

An Android app that redraws the seals of the Goetia from the Lesser Key of Solomon and leads the operation in order.

ContactThe spirits of the Goetia

The Lemegeton; the Clavicula Salomonis

Find it on Google PlayAndroid · binaural edition · EEG add-on coming

What the app does

Redraws each spirit’s seal from the manuscripts, sets the circle and triangle, and leads the conjuration, charge and discharge in order.

Ways in: The seal and the gaze, The Name, repeated, Chant and drone. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The Goetia is the most famous grimoire in the West and the most misread. Its operation is long, formal and repetitive: hours of prayer and conjuration before any appearance is expected. That length is a clue. The rite works on the operator first.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Lemegeton; the Clavicula Salomonis. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
