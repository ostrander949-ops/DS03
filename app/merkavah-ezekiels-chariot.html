Merkavah: Ezekiel’s Chariot

A guided Merkavah meditation that builds Ezekiel’s vision of the chariot-throne element by element.

ContactThe vision of the chariot

Ezekiel 1 and 10; the Merkavah tradition

Get it on Google PlayAndroid · binaural edition · EEG add-on coming

What the app does

Builds Ezekiel’s vision element by element: the storm wind from the north, the four living creatures, the wheels within wheels full of eyes, the crystal firmament, the likeness of a man upon the throne. Each element is held in contemplation before the next is added.

Ways in: Ascent, The seal and the gaze, Binaural entrainment. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The rabbis restricted study of the chariot to the few who were ready; it was treated as the most direct approach to God and the most perilous. The recreation uses the vision as its readers did, as a fixed sequence of images that gathers all attention to a single point.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
Ezekiel 1 and 10; the Merkavah tradition. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
