What the app does

A live star map of your own sky with the historical correspondences laid over it as clearly marked layers: the angels of the hours, the intelligences of the planets, the older Mesopotamian and Egyptian assignments. A ritual sky planner finds when a chosen power stands highest above you.

Ways in: The hour, The seal and the gaze, Binaural entrainment. Every session is carried on binaural sound tuned toward theta, the slow rhythm of the edge of sleep that these rites were built to reach. More on the state.

Why the rite works

The Renaissance magicians did not separate astronomy from contact. To speak to a planetary intelligence you faced where the planet actually stood. The Veil keeps the astronomy exact and the tradition labelled as tradition, so you know precisely what the sky is doing and precisely what the sources claimed about it.

Questions

Do I need anything besides my phone?
Headphones. The app carries the rite on binaural sound, which only works when each ear hears its own tone.
What is it based on?
The Heptameron, the Ars Paulina and Agrippa; Mesopotamian and Egyptian star lore. The names, words and order are taken from the source and guided step by step. The full list of sources is in the Library.
Is there an EEG version?
Not yet. EEG neurofeedback editions are coming as a $19.99 add-on per app, built in order of request. They will need a Neurosity Crown headset, sold separately.
